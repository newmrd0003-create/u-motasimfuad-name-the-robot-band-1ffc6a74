One funny name for a band made of robots, plus a few words on its hit song, all under 200 characters. The program writes the entry to `entry.txt`.

## Entry

> The Captchas — hit single ‘Select All Traffic Lights’ peaked at #2 after the band failed its own captcha three times.

## Why this angle

The only other entry leans on a computer-crash joke (Ctrl+Alt, 404, blue screen). This one takes a different road: the band is named after the test itself, and its hit song is the test's task — then the band fails its own test three times. A robot band that cannot pass a captcha is a joke with two layers, and "peaked at #2" turns the failure into a near-miss chart story instead of a flat flop. It reads like a band that could exist, and the joke lands on the first read with no explanation needed.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
